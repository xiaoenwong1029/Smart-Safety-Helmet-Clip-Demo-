🛡 Heat-Risk Management & Evidence

1. Identify areas for reviewUse repeated elevated environmental readings to flag locations for further assessment by competent site personnel.
2. Record operational actionsLink findings with verified WBGT surveys, work/rest controls, hydration measures and follow-up actions in future development.
3. Preserve traceable evidenceRetain timestamps, locations, device status, validation records and responsible-person approvals for internal climate risk reviews.
Potential sustainability relevance: After validation and governance review, environmental trends and heat-risk action records could support assessment and monitoring of climate-related physical risks relevant to NSRF / IFRS S2. This dashboard does not generate compliant disclosure statements or measure individual heat stress.
